Tatoeba data is attributed to its contributors under CC BY 2.0 France:
https://tatoeba.org/ and https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/.
Only derived frequency lists are distributed. Atypoi trains an in-memory English trigram
model from the bundled SCOWL list and combines Korean corpus counts with frequency tiers.
There is no dependency on macOS dictionaries or Japanese engines. FrequencyWords data is
not included in this version.

The build uses LLVM-MinGW (LLVM/Clang, LLD, MinGW-w64 and libc++).
The runtime notices from the pinned toolchain are included in packaged distributions.
